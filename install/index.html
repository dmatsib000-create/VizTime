TimeFall

Put TimeFall on your phone

A calm visual timer for time blindness and transitions. It is free, keeps everything on your own phone, and has no account to make.

iPhoneThrough TestFlight, Apple’s app for trying apps before they are in the store

  1. Install the free TestFlight app from the App Store (it is by Apple).
  2. Then, on the phone, open this link: Get TimeFall in TestFlightThe iPhone link is being set up. Check back here soon.
  3. In TestFlight, tap Accept, then Install. TimeFall appears on your Home Screen like any app.

TestFlight updates TimeFall on its own. Every three months Apple asks TestFlight to fetch a fresh copy; if TimeFall ever says it has expired, open TestFlight and tap Update.

AndroidFrom a download, outside the Play Store

  1. On the phone, download the app: Download TimeFall for Android
  2. Open the downloaded file (from the notification, or your Files app). If the phone says this source isn’t allowed to install apps, tap Settings and allow it for your browser or Files app, then go back and try again.
  3. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect asks about an app it doesn’t know, choose Install anyway: it is a small app from a friend, not from a store.

When a newer TimeFall exists, the app says so on its first screen, with a link back here. Download the file again and open it: it installs over the old one, and your presets and notes stay.

Any phone or computerWithout installing anything

TimeFall also works as a web page: dmatsib000-create.github.io/VizTime. On an iPhone, Safari’s Share button offers Add to Home Screen. The web page can’t remind you while the phone is locked; the apps above can.

Already using the web page? In it, open Menu → Your data → Copy a code, then on the same phone tap Open in the TimeFall app, and your presets and settings come along.

Everything you type stays on your phone. TimeFall has no account, no server and no tracking. More in the guide.
